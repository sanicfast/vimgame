Vim Cheatsheet

Modes

Esc→ Normal mode
iInsert before cursor
aAppend after cursor
oOpen line below
OOpen line above

Movement

h / ← / ⌫Left
j / ↓Down
k / ↑Up
l / →Right
wNext word
bBeginning of word
0Start of line
$End of line
ggTop of file
GBottom of file

Operators

d{mot}Delete motion
ddDelete line
diwDelete inner word
dawDelete a word (with space)
ciwChange inner word
cawChange a word (with space)
ccChange line
yyYank line
yw/y$/...Yank motion
yiwYank inner word
pPaste after cursor
PPaste before cursor
xDelete char

History

uUndo
3uUndo 3 times
Ctrl+RRedo
.Repeat last change
3.Repeat 3 times

Notes

Operators combine with motions and text objects (e.g. dw, ciw). Most mutations are repeatable with ..
